        How to play

        The game

        Hear a song, then pick its title from four choices — the faster you name that tune, the more points you score.

        1. Add players (optional): type each name and press Add. Up to players take turns in the order shown; press × to remove one. Leave it empty to play solo.
        2. Pick a category, or type any artist, genre or decade and press Add to save it as a new category.
        3. Choose the rounds. With two or more players this is rounds each, so everyone gets the same number of turns.
        4. Press Start game. Each round opens with a burst on the play button and a -second countdown, then the song starts by itself.
        5. Pick the song as soon as you know it. Get it right and the album cover lights up; get it wrong and you score nothing for that round, but the song keeps playing so you can hear what it was.
        6. Press Next for the next round (the screen tells you whose turn it is). After the last round you'll see the winner, the final standings and a round-by-round list.

        The results screen also keeps Tonight's scoreboard: every game played in this browser tab, with running totals. It clears when you close the tab, or press Reset totals. Songs you've already heard this session are avoided in later games.

        Keyboard: Space plays or stops the song (after you've answered), and 1–4 pick an answer.

        Scoring

        The clock is how much of the song has played when you answer. It doesn't start until the music does, so the countdown never costs you anything.

        Correct answer withinPoints

        The live score under the play button counts down in red as the song plays. The most you can score in a game is points per round.

        Sound problems

        No music on the first screen Browsers don't allow sound until you interact with the page. Tap anywhere, or the ♪ Tap for music button. If it says ♪ Music off, tap it to turn music back on.
        No sound on an iPhone or iPad Check the volume, and that the ringer switch (or Action button) isn't set to silent — on iOS older than 16.4, silent mode mutes the game. Also check sound isn't going to Bluetooth headphones you're not wearing.
        "Your browser blocked autoplay. Press ▶ to start the song." Your browser didn't let the song start on its own. Press ▶ and the round carries on as normal.
        "Couldn't load this song" That song's preview wasn't available or the connection dropped, so the round is skipped and nobody loses points. The game streams songs from Apple Music, so it needs an internet connection.
